## Platform
Web, desktop-first collaborative design workspace.

## Users and purpose
Designers collaborate with people and Mits (AI agents) to evolve a design, review outputs, and revisit earlier iterations. The main application connects projects, task conversations and assets to the existing reading workspace.

## Confirmed model — 2026-09-25
- Dialog is a continuing task. All tasks have equal hierarchy within their project. Builder means creator, who can be the current user, another person or a Mit.
- Inbox filters: All, Mits, Co-workers. Human messages may be unassigned to a task.
- Input and Output attachment cards stay at the conversation's upper right. Clicking an attachment opens a resizable, closable, expandable reading pane.
- Projects organize instructions, knowledge, outputs, collaborators and dialogs.
- Task states: To do, In progress, Ready for review, Complete. Review is a human decision.
- Initial scope: a functional frontend demonstration, not a connected AI or messaging backend.
- Preserve the existing Iteration Gallery and Version stream, including their standalone operation.

## Brand commitments
Use the user-supplied Brand/mitwerk-light-dark-design-system.html palette, with incumbent reading-pane density and Neurial Grotesk typography. No inherited Stage Brief, Grill Me or mandatory stage approvals.

Human collaboration and project management (2026-09-27): Home aggregates attention across the workspace. Projects lists independent projects with their own members, instructions and tasks. Co-workers provides human discovery, direct messaging and project creation. Direct messages need no task. Shared files appear in messages and open the incumbent reader. Sample colleagues, responses and membership are local demo data.

Mit customization (2026-09-28): Users can create and edit named AI collaborators with roles, working instructions, skills, tool preferences and review triggers. Stable IDs preserve references across renames. A saved profile can start a project task or an Inbox task. All configuration persists locally; no live agent runtime is connected.
